The Lore of Gooby

Once upon a swamp, Gooby left his woeful past behind, found a home among the trees, and gorged himself on the most delicious fruits he'd ever eaten.

His whole philosophy fits in a speech bubble: "Life is good!" A little weird, a little existential — exactly the energy a memecoin needs.

"Bless my sad soul! What a strange place this is!" — Gooby, wandering into a swamp more wonderful and weird than anything in his little books.

Storybook landscapes, a shy tadpole who loved books more than swimming, and jokes that get weirdly philosophical. That's the flavor of $GOOBY — and somehow, still: life is good.

Where the frog comes from

Roots of the Frog

Read the interview on LA TACO ↗

Matt Furie created Pepe the Frog in 2005 for his comic Boy's Club. While Robert Crumb's underground 1960s comics and various pop-culture styles influenced Furie's overall artistic aesthetic, Pepe himself was an original creation rather than an adaptation of a specific character named "Gooby."

$GOOBY simply tips its hat to that scrappy, hand-inked underground lineage — a weird little frog with a big, cozy heart.
